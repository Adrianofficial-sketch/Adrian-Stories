The countless Murder 



My name is Jackson. I’m 15, and I live in the kind of town where nothing ever happens.

Nothing. Ever.

Until yesterday

I was walking home from school, half scrolling my phone, half ignoring the world, when I froze.

There were police cars parked right outside Mrs. Sophia’s house.

Everyone knows Mrs. Sophia.

She’s the lady who gives little money for charities, and leaves bowls of food out for the stray cats. She’s nice. Quiet. The kind of person you’d like to know.

But today, her front door was wide open, and a group of police was standing there like they was guarding something awful.

My friend Mia ran up and grabbed my arm so tight it hurt. Her voice was all shaky.

“Did you hear? Mrs. Sophia… she’s hurt really bad. Like… really bad.”

My stomach just dropped. I felt sick, like I was gonna throw up.

I stared at her porch.

And then I saw it.

Under the doormat, half-sticking out, something shiny.

A tiny silver bracelet.

I walk past this house every single morning.

It wasn’t there before.

The police were busy. The neighbors were whispering. No one was looking at the doormat.

No one but me.

I stared at that little bracelet, and my head felt dizzy.

I knew it right then.

I wasn’t just gonna stand here and watch.

I was gonna figure out what really happened.

After the police officially announced that the case had been closed, the house stood quiet and completely empty. I went over to take one last look around, and that’s when I realized the bracelet was gone.

I was stunned, because my friend Grace wore that kinda bracelet, I rushed over to her house, she looked at me, having a look of uneasy and a anxious look, said she don’t have that bracelet.

I quickly rushed to call my friend Mia, she came over, with a confused look, what’s happening she said, is it urgent?

My nerves were dangling, yes, I said, I think that my friend Grace was in the part where Mrs. Sophia’s murder is, she wore the bracelet that is under the doormat, you didn’t see it, didn’t you?

We both set on without another word, we secretly went to Grace’s house, and went into the dark and chilling basement, there, we searched and searched, and saw a bracelet, the same one, covered with a cloth full of blood. Me and Mia gasped, there, was full of guns and knives, we quickly put the bloody cloth and the bracelet into a plastic bag and went to the police station.

The police later found out some breathtakingly terrifying news the blood was mixed from two people, not Mrs. Sophia, but two missing students from the primary school who disappeared two years ago. We were shocked. Did Grace kill three people? We couldn’t believe it, but the handprints also don’t match Grace’s fingerprints, they match an unknown person.

That unknown person was also believed to vanish several years ago, which made it even more impossible for them to be the one responsible for killing three people now. Jackson and Mia was confused, had the police mixed up and had the wrong fingerprint?

I went back home, and Mia went back to her house too. 

I couldn’t sleep. I kept thinking about the blood, and a question popped into my head, How can a vanished person kill three people and leave a fingerprint that the police can’t use to track him or her down, with no evidence?

That moment lead me into deep thinking.

Was there a twin?

How can he or she do that?

I drifted to sleep. 

I went to school the next morning with a cranky and tired attitude, thinking, when the news announced that another person had gone missing. I went to the police, telling them to give me permission to investigate and inspect people. There are three people, the first one said that he didn’t know anything about the murder, the second person said he thinks it was a coincidence, the third one said he thinks that the murder might’ve been a long time ago, which makes no sense. So who is suspicious?

The third person is the most suspicious. He mentioned “murder” before it was confirmed, proving he knew details no ordinary person should have known.

I rushed back with the police, and this time, we did found some valuable evidence, he said that the people that were killed were  not in the same country, that means the killer of these people is also racist.

Jackson collected these information and don’t seem to lead to anything. Me and Mia went to Grace’s house and the police arrested her for killing Mrs Sophia after she admitted it, but who in the world killed those students?

There was a quiet neighbor who was always watching chaos but never spoke a thing, Mia who was a fast thinker, said that the neighbor might be involved by the disappearance of those students, I agreed with Mia, and we went to the neighbors house.

We knocked on his door, he surprisingly opened it and asked us to leave immediately, I said, we just want to ask some questions, can we do it? Mia nodded, awaiting the answer, the neighbor let us in, we sat by the kitchen table, which we had a weird feeling.

The house was oddly clean. No family photos, no dust, just plain, new furniture. This neighbor had lived here for years. How could his furniture still be in such good condition? We talked for a while. He spoke shakily, like he was about to cry.

Suddenly, we heard banging coming from under the floor.The neighbor stood up quickly and said it was just a plumbing problem.But Mia suspected something was very wrong.He quickly offered us glasses of water, told us to drink it, then went to the toilet.I immediately grabbed both glasses and splashed the water down the sink.That was when I saw some blood on the bottom of one glass.Mia and I quickly took a photo and sat back down just in time, right before the neighbor came back.

We said goodbye to the neighbor and ran to the police station.Could Jack(neighbor) be the killer?He was 34 years old, and had never done anything wrong before.That was when Jack started fleeing the country.

The police ran to his house, but it was too late, he already burned all his furniture and 
Got the suspect with him, inside the basement, was full of chains and blood, with a stick that was severely broken.

The police ordered all planes to land so no one could leave the country, but the sneaky killer had already fled on a boat.  
Mia and I both knew this was the real killer.

After a few days, the police informed us that Jack had been caught—but he wasn’t the real killer.Jack said there was a group that used his house as a torture room, and he was part of that group.So the police sent him to jail.

I stayed up all night thinking. Jack was just a goon. There was still a real killer and a whole team out there. We had to find out who was really in charge before someone else got hurt or dead.

The police kept searching for the killer’s team, but they didn’t find a single clue, not even a small hint. Mia and I were confused and frustrated, but we refused to give up. We kept investigating, determined to find the truth.

We found a clue in Jack’s grey jacket, we found a notebook and a photo of the whole team, Mia copied down the information, while I saw that the team always travel to many places, and had a whole community, the leader was called 2x, it was his code name, but we didn’t know anything more.

We told the police that they traveled to Columbia and Egypt before to smuggle drugs and torture rich people for the money. The police informed the FBI and they send some teams of cops there. Me and Mia was sure they will be caught this time.

But the smuggler-torture people weren’t that easy, they have spies everywhere, which tell the boss that the FBI was tracking them, so they again traveled to Brazil.
