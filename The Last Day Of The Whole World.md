The Last Day Of The Whole World

I realized that something is weird going on, I accidentally dropped a bunch of marbles when I was cleaning my apartment and noticed it was heading south, I moved in a couple of days ago, and this building is new, how can the floor be not straight? I told the proprietor that the floor is not straight, but he just said it might be a mistake, I tried it again, but it still rolled to south.

After work one day, I saw there are some deep cracks on the floor beside the building, I remembered that they weren’t there in the morning. The next second, RUMBLE! The whole building collapsed inside a big crack and a enormous hole emerged, it swallowed the whole building, at that moment, cracks suddenly emerged beside me and hot lava was spurting from the ground! I quickly ran to the nearest gas station and stole a man’s car, at that point, I knew, it was the end of the world.

I raced to the airport and secretly sneaked into the baggage area, I hid in a big and soft bag that is filled with balloons for no reason. After the airplane took off, I told my parents about the news, they are in California and I’m in England, now going to…

CHINA???

After I went there, I was finally relaxed, my friend is a weather-Geography person, and he is a professor. I told him about the news, his face turned pale INSTANTLY, he knew this, it was said to be the end of the world and the universe, he quickly opened his computer and start working and researching about this horrible disaster.

He suddenly threw his laptop on the ground, shouting, he said it was a dark energy that is emitting radioactive vibrations from a star that is about to become a black hole, we instantly went to our basement hideout, and started our own private rocket  in case of a apocalypse. It still needed 3 days to go, we opened the TV, and it said that the earth is now experiencing weird moving of the continent plates and extreme high rising temperatures from the core of the earth, the news person said it’s best to hide in our houses and gather as much food as we can, we closed the TV, we were sure.

We finally started the engine of our rocket, and it’s starting to take off. 10, 9 8 7 6…
